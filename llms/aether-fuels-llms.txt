# Aether Fuels

> Aether Fuels is a sustainable-fuels technology company headquartered in Singapore with a
> U.S. R&D center on the GTI Energy campus near Chicago. Its Aether Aurora process converts
> waste carbon — CO2, CO, methane and other hydrocarbons from gasified biomass, municipal
> solid waste, biogas and industrial off-gases — into drop-in sustainable liquid fuels for
> aviation and ocean shipping.

## Technology

- Aether Aurora: a radically simplified evolution of industrial gas-to-liquid, licensed in
  part from strategic partner GTI Energy.
- Feedstock flexibility: any feedstock convertible to CO, CO2, CH4 and/or H2.
- Electrified syngas generation: a novel electric thermochemical reactor for higher yield,
  higher energy efficiency and a smaller reactor footprint.
- Intensified syngas generation and upgrading: three novel catalysts that reduce equipment
  count through process intensification.
- Scale today: a 1.5 gallon-per-day fully integrated pilot plant with GTI Energy, operational
  since 2021; a 1+ barrel-per-day integrated demonstration plant under construction.
